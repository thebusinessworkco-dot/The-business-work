## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an aesthetic of architectural restraint, deliberate proportion, and timeless digital craftsmanship for an enterprise-tier AI automation and systems studio. The visual language rejects ephemeral tech tropes—such as neon glows, aggressive gradients, decorative drop-shadows, and floating translucent orbs—in favor of structural discipline, high editorial precision, and quiet luxury.

The experience evokes the feeling of an archival monograph or an architectural blueprint realized through digital interfaces: confident, unhurried, razor-sharp, and intrinsically authoritative. The target audience encompasses c-suite technology leaders, institutional operators, and design-literate founders who prioritize engineering durability, operational clarity, and institutional prestige.

### Style Core
- **Architectural Minimalism:** Uncompromised grid alignment, generous macro-whitespace, and meticulous structural hierarchy.
- **Typographic Rigor:** Swiss typographic rhythm paired with quiet, high-contrast serif accents to imbue bespoke studio intellect.
- **Subtle Tactility:** Low-contrast hairline rules, chalk-stone surfaces, and whisper-quiet micro-interactions over loud transitions.

## Colors

The palette references gallery spaces, archival paper stock, and patinated minerals. It avoids sterile clinical whites and harsh pure blacks, using warm mineral tones that reduce optical fatigue while maintaining crisp contrast ratios compliant with WCAG AAA standards for editorial and data components.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas / Ground (`#F8F7F4`):** Warm bone/chalk stone base that defines the macro environment.
- **Surface Layer (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white reserved for elevated architectural cards, data modules, and focal surfaces.
- **Primary Ink (`#111110`):** Carbon-neutral deep ink for primary titles, essential metrics, and authoritative interactions.
- **Secondary Ink (`#575653`):** Warm muted basalt for secondary copy, captions, and structural annotations.
- **Primary Accent (`#283730`):** Deep pine mineral, used sparingly for active states, confirmed automation sequences, and strategic focal points.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#7A7970`):** Olive taupe for metadata tags, secondary borders, and subtle metric labels.
- **Border / Hairline (`#E5E3DC`):** Architectural boundary line used at 1px thickness across all dividers and surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes two precise typefaces: **Space Grotesk** for display headlines, architectural titles, data callouts, and technical indicators, paired with **Manrope** for continuous editorial reading and complex interface densities.

### Rules & Formatting
- **Tracking Discipline:** Headings employ tight, negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) to project architectural density. Labels, indicators, and metadata tags invert this convention with generous positive tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) in uppercase format.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Space Grotesk's disciplined geometric curves provide crisp technological modernism, while Manrope balances high legibility with human warmth across complex automated workflows.
- **Monospace Elements:** Inline system telemetry, pipeline hashes, and code metrics inherit standard tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure absolute alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored by a strict 12-column grid system derived from international typographic standards. Alignment is mathematical and visible; structural modules sit flush against grid boundaries demarcated by 1px rules rather than disconnected floating islands.

### Responsive Cadence
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Max container bound of 1440px with balanced horizontal margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Complex modular tables reflow into stacked twin-column arrays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Deep macro spacing scales down cleanly via mobile font and margin tokens.

### Spacing Application
- Component internal padding strictly follows mathematical multiples of 4px/8px using `space-xs` through `space-xl`.
- Inter-section spacing prioritizes expansive vertical breath: major structural blocks are separated by `space-xl` or larger rhythmic bands (4rem–6rem), honoring the quiet-luxury requirement of generous negative space.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews diffuse drop-shadows, directional blurs, and pseudo-skeuomorphic depth. Hierarchy is established strictly through **tonal surfacing**, **hairline structure**, and **spatial layering**.

### Spatial Techniques
- **Zero-Shadow Rule:** Default UI modules, cards, dropdowns, and dialogs utilize `box-shadow: none`. 
- **Hairline Division:** Hierarchy is manifested through crisp 1px borders (`#E5E3DC`). When a card hovers or gains focus, the border transitions cleanly to `#283730` or `#111110` with zero movement or artificial glow.
- **Surface Elevation:**
  - *Layer 0 (Canvas):* `#F8F7F4` provides the foundational stone canvas.
  - *Layer 1 (Card/Section):* `#FFFFFF` serves as structural content modules, neatly bounded by hairline borders.
  - *Layer 2 (Overlays/Drawers):* `#FFFFFF` bounded by a 1px border (`#111110`), seated above the canvas with a solid 1px architectural divider or an unblurred semi-opaque scrim (`rgba(17, 17, 16, 0.3)`).

## Shapes

The geometric identity is defined by sharp precision (`roundedness: 0`). Sharp 90-degree corners express structural permanence, architectural intent, and uncompromising engineering clarity.

### Geometric Rules
- All cards, buttons, input fields, badges, tabs, and modals carry `border-radius: 0px`.
- Circular forms are strictly isolated to system status indicators (e.g., active pipeline pulse dots) and avatars.
- Interactive states rely on internal hairline shifts, fill inversions, or typographic changes rather than corner deformation or scale morphing.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#111110` background, `#F8F7F4` uppercase text (`label-md`), 0px border-radius, `padding: 0.875rem 1.75rem`. On hover: transition background to `#283730` (deep pine) over 180ms ease.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1px solid border (`#E5E3DC`), `#111110` text. On hover: border color darkens to `#111110`, background shifts to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Text Link / Action:** Unadorned typography with a continuous 1px underline positioned 4px below baseline. Hover state subtly changes text to `#283730`.

### Cards & Modular Containers
- Constructed on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a continuous 1px border (`#E5E3DC`).
- Content within cards adheres to rigorous internal padding (`space-lg` desktop, `space-md` mobile).
- Cards in an automated pipeline grid sit directly adjacent to one another, sharing 1px borders to emulate architectural floorplans and matrix consoles.

### Form Inputs & Select Fields
- **Container:** Rectangular box, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border (`#E5E3DC`), 0px radius, `padding: 0.75rem 1rem`.
- **Typography:** Value rendered in `body-md` (`#111110`); placeholder in `body-md` (`#7A7970`).
- **Focus State:** 1px border shifts sharply to `#111110` with no external halo or ring.
- **Field Labels:** Positioned above the field in `label-sm` uppercase Space Grotesk (`#575653`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkbox:** 16px square, 0px radius, 1px border (`#7A7970`). Checked state fills solid `#111110` with a sharp white architectural checkmark icon.
- **Radio Button:** 16px square or strict circle with 1px border (`#7A7970`). Selected state reveals an inner sharp dot in `#111110`.

### Tags, Chips & Status Indicators
- **Metadata Tag:** `#F8F7F4` background with a 1px border (`#E5E3DC`), `label-sm` Space Grotesk uppercase text (`#575653`), `padding: 0.25rem 0.5rem`.
- **System Status Indicator:** 6px solid dot (`#283730` for active/healthy, `#7A7970` for idle/queued) accompanied by inline `label-sm` text.

### Architectural Pipeline Node (Specialized Component)
- Distinct modular container for displaying AI logic paths and data pipelines.
- Displays step numbers in `label-sm` (`#7A7970`), process name in `headline-sm`, and latency/metric status in monospaced `body-sm`. Modules interconnect via strict 1px horizontal and vertical hairpins in `#E5E3DC`.
